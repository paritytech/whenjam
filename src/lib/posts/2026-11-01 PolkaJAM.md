Most Gray Paper 0.8.1 changes are now implemented in PolkaJAM, including:
- a dedicated compile hostcall for inner PVM modules
- bounded preimage requests
- the new Thin VRF scheme

The [new auditing tranche mechanism was designed](https://github.com/gavofyork/graypaper/pull/552), implemented and opened for review.
