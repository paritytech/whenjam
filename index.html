Progress tracking
on the
Join-Accumulate Machine

Abstract. This site exists to share progress on JAM — the Join-Accumulate Machine — a mostly-coherent trustless supercomputer:

  • PolkaJAM (a Parity's full node Rust implementation of JAM) will be production ready by end of Q1 2027, and initialy deployed to create a JAM testnet. In lock step with the Polkadot migration (from the Relay Chain) to the Parachain Service, JAM will be launched in production.
  • The Parachain Service (the replacement of the Polkadot Relay Chain) will be production ready, before the end of Q2 2027. The Parachain Service will not only provide a replacement for the Relay Chain, it will also enable the the tokenomics of JAM resource tokens from Polkadot Hub.
  • The Minimal Relay project will complete the work started with AHMv1, i.e. migrate the remaining balances and non-system calls off the Polkadot Relay Chain (before end of Q1 2027), so unblocking the Polkadot migration from the Relay Chain to Parachain Service.
